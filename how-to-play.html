How to Play

You were taking your nightly stroll when you suddenly heard laughter echoing in the distance. Curious, you followed the sound and stumbled upon towering, rusty silver gates surrounding a mysterious circus. The place looked abandoned, as if no one had entered in years. Strangely, you pass this path every day, but you've never seen it before. Intrigued, you push the gates open and step inside. You had no idea about the terror that was about to unfold...

Welcome to "Guess That Number!" — an interactive guessing game. Your goal is to guess a number within a given range:

  • Easy: Smaller range, more lives.
  • Medium: Moderate range, fewer lives.
  • Hard: Larger range, fewest lives.

Each time you guess correctly, you'll earn a star. Collect three stars to win the game. But beware: for every wrong guess, you lose a life. If you lose all your lives, the game resets, and you'll face the horrors of the circus again. Are you brave enough to beat the game, or will the circus claim you?
